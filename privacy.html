Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Air Sprout ("we") handles data accessed through our internal reporting tools ("the app").

What the app accesses

When an authorised Air Sprout user signs in with Google, the app requests read-only access to Google Ads, Google Analytics and Google Search Console data for accounts that user already has permission to view. This includes campaign, traffic and search performance data, such as impressions, clicks, costs, conversions and search queries.

How we use it

Data is used only to review, analyse and report on advertising and website performance for Air Sprout and its clients. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising purposes.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the authorised user's own device. Data is fetched on request and not stored on any Air Sprout server. Reports prepared for clients are shared only with that client.

Google API Services

Air Sprout's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
